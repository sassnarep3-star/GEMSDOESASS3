## Project charter (read before each research session)

**Objective:** build an auditable, reproducible workflow for discovering previously unmapped faults in the DOE GEMS/GeoDAWN region, evaluate hypotheses with spatially blocked hide-and-recover validation, and only then produce a competition-format GeoTIFF. Maximize the probability of a real, defensible improvement; own and report negative results.

**Non-negotiable protocol**

1. Work only in the method lane explicitly assigned for the session. Before placement and on final predictions, compare against the complete aligned registry. Stop and record a duplicate if any surface rank correlation is greater than 0.90 or more than 70% of emitted dots lie within 3 pixels of a registry raster. Never move dots merely to evade this gate.
2. Reuse the shared cached features, holdout evaluator, and submission writer. Hold out whole fault segments with a buffer; derive catalogue features from visible faults only; mask visible faults pixel-exactly; evaluate pooled DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8, and the 300 m triangular kernel. Fix shared tools in their template, not in a private fork.
3. Label every reported score as either **HOLDOUT-DTI** (evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI) or **ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED** (transcribed from a submission receipt). Never call a projection a score. A leaderboard claim without a receipt is unverified.
4. Run each feature alone through the holdout leakage canary. AUC above 0.90 is leakage until explained and proven otherwise.
5. Do no more than 3 experiments or 2 hours, whichever comes first. A slot decision is separate from research validation and must respect the current competition cap.
6. A downloadable artifact is not automatically eligible to submit. Fail closed unless holdout, leakage, novelty, format, and selector/slot gates all pass. The required GeoTIFF contract is one float32 band, EPSG:32611, matching the official grid/transform, in-footprint values in [0,1], and null/NaN outside the footprint.
7. End every run with a JSON card recording hypothesis, mechanism, plausible non-fault mimic, HOLDOUT-DTI and CI (or null), registry correlation/overlap (or null), raster SHA-256 (or null), validator result, proposed name/note, and `promote` or `negative` verdict.

## Verified starting point

The official problem page says the target is geological faults indicative of geothermal resources, that the public fault catalogue is incomplete and may contain inaccuracies, and that expert-identified new faults form the initial test set. It lists provided geophysical, strain, elevation, and earthquake-density features. Its metric is distance-weighted Tversky with α=0.2, β=0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. The official submission specification requires EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, matching bounds, one float32 band with predictions in [0,1], and null/NaN outside the bounds.
