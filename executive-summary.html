Why did 0.2778 score highest?

The official metric is a distance-weighted Tversky index; the official challenge page sets a 300 m triangular support and alpha=0.2, beta=0.8. False negatives have a larger coefficient than false positives. A plausible explanation is that this raster's spatial coverage happened to match hidden faults while limiting false-positive mass. Pruning redundant predictions could also help, but could remove true unmapped faults. The hidden labels, per-pixel error components, exact upload bytes and organizer receipt are unavailable here; therefore neither causal mechanism is demonstrated.
