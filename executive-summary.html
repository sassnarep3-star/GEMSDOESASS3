BEFORE A DOWNLOAD

What has to pass?

01

Lane & data provenance

An explicitly assigned method, the shared cached features and a complete, refreshed registry are required. The present prompt refers to a method paragraph that is not included; the cache is absent.

02

Independent, leak-safe evidence

Hide whole buffered fault segments, derive catalogue features from visible faults only, mask visible fault pixels exactly, run every feature-alone leakage canary, and compute pooled HOLDOUT-DTI with a paired 95% spatial-block confidence interval against the comparable best. No leaderboard estimate is inferred without validated calibration.

03

Novelty on the surface and the dots

Compare against every registry raster before placement and after emission. Stop if rank correlation exceeds 0.90 or more than 70% of candidate dots fall within three pixels of one registry raster. Do not exempt the lattice or move dots to evade the rule.

04

Reopened bytes and separate selection

Validate the exact saved single-band float32 TIFF, range and grid against an authenticated template. A positive research result is not a slot: a separate selector checks all gates and the current portal allowance.

WHEN A FILE IS ACTUALLY CLEARED

How to make a submission

  1. Check the current decision and quota. Ensure the exact candidate and hash have a positive research verdict and separate selector approval. The published rules say three submissions per week; verify the current allowance on the submission page because it may change.
  2. Download the cleared TIFF. Use its one-click link only after it exists; check that the SHA-256 matches its run card. Do not rename or resubmit a previously scored file to manufacture uniqueness.
  3. Open DrivenData. Sign in, open the GEMS competition, choose “Submit,” then “Make new submission.”
  4. Choose the TIFF and note. Upload the single GeoTIFF (or a ZIP containing only that TIFF). Copy the candidate's own short note, at most 140 characters. There is no name or note to copy until a new candidate exists.
  5. Save the receipt. Record the date, name, note, local SHA-256 and organizer-returned score or rejection together. A local format pass is not organizer acceptance.

Official rules require disclosure of generative AI use in the submission narrative and reproducible solution assets for finalists. Read the rules and verify eligibility and external-data rights.

TROUBLESHOOTING

“Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

The rejected file and organizer diagnosis are not available, so the exact cause is unknown. Out-of-range values, NaNs, infinities and negative nodata sentinels are possibilities, not a diagnosis. Inspect the exact file, not just the in-memory model output:

python scripts/geotiff_audit.py candidate.tif \
  --template /path/to/authenticated_sample_submission.tif

The audit reports the inside-footprint range, mask, raw whole-array range, CRS, shape and affine transform separately. It never silently clips values. It also detects external mask sidecars that would not travel in a single-file upload. Its exit code is zero only when both local profiles pass; that conservative check is not organizer approval.

Important ambiguity: the official problem page specifies null/NaN outside the footprint, while the reported error raises a concern about raw-array range checks. An internal mask with finite raw outside values can satisfy both local tests, but only the organizer can confirm how its reader treats that encoding. Do not claim it guarantees portal acceptance.

WHY WE STOPPED

A uniqueness rule with a hard bound

The pinned owner-mirrored lattice leaves just 6,649 footprint pixels farther than three pixels from a lattice dot. For any 37,654 distinct in-footprint dots, at least 31,005 must overlap its halo: 82.34%, above the strict 70% stop. At this budget no placement can pass that witness. A smaller budget is not automatically unique or high-scoring.

Our independent spot checks found literal lattice overlaps of 99.61%, 99.62%, 99.17% and 99.78% for highlighted GEMSDOE27, GEMSDOE28, GEMSDOE36 and newer GEMSDOE24 files respectively. The smaller GEMSDOE24 file also fails by measurement, not by the standard-budget bound. Do not rely on narrower promotion claims. Inspect the calculations and SHA-256 pins.
