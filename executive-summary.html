EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What we found.

Across the nine pinned historical supports, every file has more than 70% of its dots within three pixels of at least one of the other eight. The GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 supports are identical. This is a nine-file analysis, not a complete registry search or approval to reuse any file.

The reported 0.2778 for GEMSDOE32 H33 is the highest user-provided value in this group, but the linked file's own audit says UNSCORED and no organizer receipt authenticates the association. We confirmed H33 removes 2,545 catalogue-near pixels from GEMSDOE31 H27 and adds none. Reducing redundant prediction mass is a plausible mechanism—not proof that this file produced that score or that a similar collar will help. New truth can be close to known faults.

The one new, pre-registered H1 surface scored 0.048064 HOLDOUT-DTI (95% CI [0.044295, 0.051633]) against 0.059785 for the terrain-only control (95% CI [0.054213, 0.065646]), using gems57-pooled-hide-v2 and 60,988 withheld positives. The paired difference was −0.011721 (95% CI [−0.014326, −0.009464]). These are continuous-surface holdouts, not final-dot or leaderboard results. No comparable previous best was reproduced on this exact evaluator.

The user cited competing leaderboard highs of 0.3195 and 0.4413 without linked receipts. The official public leaderboard showed 0.4544 when observed on 27 October 2026 UTC, but it does not identify our TIFF or provide a final/private score. Check the official leaderboard for its current state. We do not automate access to DrivenData.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

What a promoted file must have.

Single bandFloating-point GeoTIFF
Matched gridEPSG:32611 · 100 m
Bounded valuesFinite [0, 1] in footprint
  1. Science: all feature-only canaries must pass, and a segment-buffered same-split holdout must beat the established same-evaluator incumbent with its paired uncertainty recorded.
  2. Novelty: against the complete accessible registry, no surface rank correlation may exceed 0.90 and no more than 70% of positive surface support or final dots may lie within three pixels of any registry raster. A single failure stops the run.
  3. Saved bytes: validate the actual file automatically, not a preview: single band, correct shape/CRS/transform, finite footprint values in [0,1], and recorded SHA-256. The supplied shared writer also enforces finite zeros outside; the official description says null/NaN outside, so verify the current organizer instructions and acceptance before assuming either policy is accepted.
  4. Separate selector: promotion and use of a live slot happen only after those gates and a current quota check. The authenticated account and quota are unavailable here.

The H1 gate failed at step 2. Its output TIFF, hash, format receipt, filename and ≤140-character submission note are intentionally absent; fabricating them would make the status unsafe.

WHEN A FUTURE CANDIDATE PASSES

Submitting, step by step.

  1. On the overview, confirm that a future decision explicitly says promote, with a unique filename, SHA-256, reproducible holdout and completed automated validator. If it says negative or lacks any evidence, stop.
  2. Download the approved TIFF from the enabled link and compare its SHA-256 with the recorded digest. The project validator should have already checked the saved file; no visual pixel inspection is required.
  3. Open the official competition page, sign in, and check the account's actual submission allowance and current rules. The website's quota display takes precedence over conflicting parenthetical rules, according to organizer guidance.
  4. Only after a separate selector authorizes a slot, upload the exact approved bytes with the unique submission name and note recorded in the run card (the note must be at most 140 characters). The present run has neither.
  5. Save the organizer receipt and link it to the raster SHA-256. Only an authenticated receipt can establish an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score for that file.

If the portal reports “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1],” do not spend another slot blindly. Re-run the automated file-level validator and check band count, finite values, nodata, all pixel values, and grid against the current official sample and portal guidance.

PROVENANCE & ACCESS

Where this leaves us.

The core rasters are hash-verified against public owner mirrors, not authenticated organizer downloads. The mirrored sample unexpectedly contains the catalogue values despite the stated all-absence example. Organizer access, data-license verification, exact receipts, full registry coverage and a same-evaluator previous best remain needed. Some sister repositories do not grant this account push access, so this site consolidates the nine reviews rather than claiming those sites were edited.
