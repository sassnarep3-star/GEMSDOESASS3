4. Why h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros scored 0.2778, and whether we can beat it

  • It is the best point on a saturated plateau, not a clever transform. The family of collar-dot rasters (GEMSDOE36 0.2750, GEMSDOE32 0.2778, GEMSDOE31/28 0.2708-0.2710, GEMSDOE25/30 0.2600) sits within 0.017 of each other while their dot counts range 37,654-44,090. Under DTI = TP/(TP+0.2 FP+0.8 FN) with a 300 m triangular kernel and the catalogue pixels excluded by the organizer, fewer dots means less FP; the champion is the smallest dot set that still keeps the kernel credit. The gap to the next file is ~1/6 of the LLI estimator's own LOO MAE, so most of it is measurement noise.
  • The metric algebra caps it. A collar at ±1/2/3 px of a true trace carries kernel weight 0.88/0.71/0.556; a pixel only enters the score if its credit beats the inclusion threshold 0.2×DTI (≈0.056 here, i.e. d<2.83 px). At zero FP, DTI = f/(0.2f+0.8), so 0.2778 means ~23.5% recall and 0.4544 means ~40% recall. Closing that gap needs ~1.7× more true positives, not re-weighting.
  • We cannot beat it with anything buildable from the provided data. Three experiments (budget exhausted): E1 geometry arms (17), E2 four ranking surfaces × masses 20k-320k px, E3/E3b nine supports and 18 selection designs. Every ranking surface built from the 19 official bands + the pinned LiDAR morphometry tops out at SGMC-CAL 0.022 vs the family's 0.061-0.064. The best design found (“λ-optimal top-2 strata”, LLI 0.2916 band [0.2696, 0.3137]) has 0.933 overlap with the family control — over the 0.70 duplicate cap, so protocol says log as duplicate and STOP rather than promote it; and its band already contains 0.2778, so even there “beating it” is not established.
  • What would beat it: genuinely new off-catalogue detections — 1 m 3DEP lidar beyond the pinned extract, InSAR/scarp change, earthquake catalogue recomputation, or the geothermal-vent priors in docs/research/ (gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391, USGS GeoDawn, GBCG INGENIOUS, NLR rules PDF). The live-fitted λ field says new expert faults are sub-parallel strands 1.4-2 px off the H19-5 trace and 5-50 px off the catalogue: that is a discovery target, not a smoothing parameter.

8. Limitations of this page

  • No organizer receipt is in this workspace for any family score; OWNER-REPORT values are what the owner typed into the standing prompt. The only organizer-scaled numbers here come from the public leaderboard render of 2026-10-30.
  • LLI is a projection fitted on 111 owner-reported live rasters; its LOO MAE (0.0221) is larger than the differences between neighbouring plateau entries. It cannot rank two files that are 0.007 apart.
  • SGMC-CAL is prevalence-calibrated against a catalogue-derived truth and is gameable in isolation (emitting that truth scores 0.5592 there while the analogous live submission scored 0.0512) — it is used as one of two holdouts, never alone.
  • Hide-and-recover 0.0 for an off-catalogue emission is a construction artefact, not evidence of failure.
  • The lane sweep sampled |Spearman| on 250k cells and used a 3-px disk; the correlation cap was not re-measured on the final 462-file sweep run (the overlap cap was, on all files).
