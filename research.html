01 / FIRST TO CONSIDER · MEDIUM COST

Opposing-flank potential-field profiles

Layers and signature. Use the provided reduced-to-pole magnetics, magnetic gradients, gravity and depth-to-conductive-base bands. Look for sign-sensitive opposing-flank profiles at multiple offsets, corroborated independently by gravity and depth.

Why a catalogue might miss it. A buried offset may leave a geophysical step without a visible surface scarp. Unfaulted lithologic contacts, dikes and magnetic remanence are important non-fault mimics.

Difference and readiness. Sign-sensitive multiscale profiles are distinct from the simple baseline, but novelty relative to all sibling work is unproven. The official problem page lists these layers; the required prepared cache is absent. Potential benefit is ranked higher than the other proposals, not quantified.

02 / ACQUISITION CONSISTENCY · HIGH COST

Line-level repeatability gate

Layers and signature. Compare detrended raw magnetic survey-line and tie-line residuals at crossings, with directional uncertainty, before gridding. Prefer anomalies repeatable across independent survey directions over stripe-like artifacts.

Why a catalogue might miss it. A concealed structural signal can persist where no outcrop is mapped. A lithologic contact is a non-fault mimic; acquisition, leveling and line artifacts can also create or erase signatures.

Difference and readiness. This tests measurement reproducibility rather than only transforming finished grids. USGS GeoDAWN lists public line CSVs and flight paths under CC0, but we did not download the archive or verify study-area coverage. Viability remains unconfirmed; potential benefit is medium.

03 / HYDROTHERMAL CONTEXT · HIGH COST

Chemistry residual at a geophysical contact

Layers and signature. Combine radiometric K/Th/U grids, geophysical contact geometry and well/spring chemistry. Evaluate a spatially cross-fitted alteration residual without using fault-derived attributes.

Why a catalogue might miss it. A permeable concealed fault may channel fluids that alter nearby material. Soil weathering, lithology, irrigation and biased sampling are non-fault alternatives.

Difference and readiness. It seeks cross-modal residual evidence rather than a single anomaly. Related hydrothermal features exist elsewhere, so global novelty is unproven. The USGS release and INGENIOUS compilation list relevant data; coverage and derived-data rights remain to be checked. Potential benefit is uncertain.

04 / TEMPORAL EVIDENCE · HIGH COST

Repeatable deformation discontinuities

Layers and signature. Compare independent multi-date Sentinel-1 interferograms and look directions for a persistent, line-like displacement break.

Why a catalogue might miss it. An active concealed structure could move without clear topography, but many real faults will show no measurable motion. Aquifer pumping, atmospheric delay and decorrelation are non-fault mimics.

Difference and readiness. This adds a temporal modality to the static inputs. Official NASA Sentinel-1 and ASF access sources were identified; authentication, scenes, coverage, sharing rights and computation have not been verified. Potential benefit is uncertain and viability is unconfirmed.
