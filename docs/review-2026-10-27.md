## Why the GEMSDOE32 claim cannot justify a higher-score promise

The official metric is distance-weighted Tversky: each prediction is a probability/confidence in `[0,1]`; nearby predictions receive partial credit through a linear triangular kernel with 300 m support; false negatives are weighted more heavily than false positives (α=0.2, β=0.8). This makes broad, carefully calibrated coverage potentially preferable to a very sparse exact-line map, but extra predictions still incur false-positive cost. The optimum depends on how candidate predictions distribute around the *unseen* expert faults and on the density/geometry of those faults. A leaderboard score alone does not reveal the raster's probabilities, locations, calibration, or false-positive/false-negative decomposition.

Consequently, the reported `0.2778` cannot tell us *why* that particular raster scored as it did without its authenticated receipt, exact TIFF bytes, evaluator implementation, and corresponding truth access. Nor does the public score establish that another strategy can exceed it. A rigorous answer requires exact artifact and receipt provenance plus an independently frozen spatial holdout that avoids catalogue leakage. Even then, holdout DTI is a proxy, not an organizer score or guaranteed private leaderboard result.

## Submission-format and safe-download note

The official submission specification requires: one GeoTIFF band, float32, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, matching bounds, probabilities/confidence in `[0,1]`, and null/NaN outside the bounds. The user-reported portal message “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” cannot be diagnosed without the rejected file bytes and portal receipt. A local validator must inspect the reopened final file, distinguish in-footprint cells from outside-footprint nodata, check that there are no in-footprint NaNs/infinities/out-of-range values, and compare CRS, shape, and affine transform to the official sample template. An all-zero fill outside is **not** asserted to satisfy the official null/NaN-outside requirement. Passing local checks is not organizer acceptance.
