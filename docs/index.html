What the estimate establishes — and what it does not

The metric is invertible. With α = 0.2 (false positives) and β = 0.8 (false negatives), substituting FP = D − TP and FN = N − TP gives

DTI = TP / (0.2·D + 0.8·N)

The denominator contains no TP term at all. The leaderboard score is therefore a linear measurement of the hidden truth set, and a linear measurement can be inverted. Every published raster with a known score states exactly how much of its mass must sit on truth:

TP_i = s_i · (0.2·D_i + 0.8·N)

The lbinv candidate was constructed from this inverse model and 128 owner-reported scores. That derivation does not establish where the hidden test faults are: no candidate-specific whole-segment spatial HOLDOUT-DTI or feature canary is recorded. Treat the 0.369 value as an ESTIMATE only; no claim that it beats the 0.2778 owner-reported reference is supported.
