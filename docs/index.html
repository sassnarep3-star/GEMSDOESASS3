The portal-range uncertainty the review flagged is now resolved

It was never an uncertainty about the competition rule; it was our own encoding.

The page 967 text says values outside the bounds may be “null or nan”, while the upload form range-tests every stored cell against [0, 1]. A raster written with NaN outside the footprint therefore fails the form with “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” even though it is format-legal on paper — reproduced on our files, 7,111,787 violations. The fix is the zeros variant: identical in-footprint assertion, 0.0 outside, no NaN anywhere. It passes the validator's portal-range test, and the organizer's scorer demonstrably accepts that encoding (the GEMSDOE32 champion at 0.2778 is itself a -zeros file). Every candidate on this site now ships both encodings, with the zeros one as the default, and scripts/validate_submission_tif.py reports portal_range_test_all_cells separately from the repo's NaN convention so the two never get confused again.
