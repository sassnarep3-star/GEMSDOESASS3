Why did 0.2778 score highest? (what the evidence supports)

  1. It is a family member, not an outlier. Measured: the GEMSDOE32 champion's dots lie within 3 px of the historical files of other focus sites at GEMSDOE24 100.0%, GEMSDOE25 100.0%, GEMSDOE27 100.0%, GEMSDOE28 99.9%, GEMSDOE30 100.0%, GEMSDOE31 100.0%, GEMSDOE33 100.0%, GEMSDOE36 100.0% (forward overlap). The nine historical focus files form one dot-field family. Differences of 0.01–0.02 between them are small placement changes.
  2. Owner scores sit in a narrow band. The nine owner-reported scores run from 0.2449 to 0.2778, a spread of 0.0329. The public leaderboard shows performance on a public subset of the test labels; the private set is used for the initial round (competition page 967). A gap of 0.01–0.02 between neighbours can therefore flip under a different split. We cannot quantify this, because the split is not published.
  3. Two holdout evaluators disagree about the champion. The best-concordance proxy, SGMC-CAL (Spearman 0.869 with owner scores, n=21), puts the champion at 0.064121, second of the nine focus files (range 0.057932–0.064444; the review's table). The nine-site pooled-hide-v2 evaluator puts the champion at 0.003885 [0.003316, 0.004478], near the bottom, and GEMSDOE24 at 0.096653. Since the two disagree, neither is established as predictive of the organizer score.
  4. A plausible mechanism, not a proven one. The test labels are new faults that expert panels mapped and that are not in the USGS fault database (competition page 967). Our holdouts withhold catalogue faults, so they measure a different target. Only an organizer receipt tests the mechanism.

How to submit once a file is cleared

  1. Only after the owner records Option A (or any other amendment) in docs/research/protocol-amendments.md and the gate is re-run. Then the file is still a candidate, not a promoted submission.
  2. A separate selector chooses one file within the weekly cap of three (NLR Official Rules §3.2, §3.4).
  3. Download the single-band GeoTIFF (or its single-file zip) and upload it through the competition's submission form. Use the submission name and note from the file's run card, which are at most 140 characters.
  4. Disclose the use of generative AI in the narrative, as the rules require.
  5. Record the score that the platform returns as ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Do not treat holdout numbers as scores.
