### Why GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 was the highest reported score

The defensible fact is only that 0.2778 is the largest **owner-reported** score among the nine entries. The repository does not contain the organizer receipt that binds that score to the TIFF hash. The exact causal reason for its score cannot be inferred from that single outcome. The reported top files are strongly related dot patterns; the registry analysis records GEMSDOE36 as containing every GEMSDOE32 champion dot and adding 1,200, yet GEMSDOE36 is reported at 0.2750. That alone shows dot count or a named physical mechanism does not explain the score.

The official competition description says initial test faults were expert-identified and absent from the public USGS database, and the final round re-scores submissions against expanded expert-reviewed labels. Thus the catalogue-recovery HOLDOUT-DTI values in this table cannot explain or estimate leaderboard performance. In particular, GEMSDOE32's low `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` value is not proof that its competition score is wrong; it measures a different target.

The old repository estimate of 0.369 for the SASS3 lbinv raster is **not an organizer score** and is not decision-grade: it is an extrapolation beyond all observed entries, uses OWNER-REPORT calibration values, has a reported ±0.10 band, and the exact TIFF contains NaN outside the footprint. The local 22-check receipt demonstrates a subset of format checks, not that DrivenData accepted it. Do not use 0.369 as a validated score estimate or reason to spend a slot.
