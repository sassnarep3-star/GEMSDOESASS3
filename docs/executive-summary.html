How to Submit to the DrivenData GEMS Competition

  1. Download the TIF above (click "Download TIF"). It is a single-band GeoTIFF matching CRS EPSG:32611, shape 3730×3292, and the sample-submission geotransform. All in-footprint values are finite and in [0, 1] with zero NaN — this avoids the "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" portal error.

It re-checks CRS/shape/transform, range [0,1], NaN, single-band, dtype float32, nodata=None, holdout-DTI vs the 0.2778 champion, leakage canary (AUC ≤ 0.90), and registry uniqueness (Spearman ≤ 0.90, within-3-px overlap ≤ 70%).
  3. Name & note: use a unique filename and a short note (≤140 characters) e.g. "clustering with k=25" so you can tell submissions apart later.
  4. Submit at DrivenData GEMS competition page → "New submission". Upload the .tif or a .zip containing it. Check the weekly submission cap on the page before uploading.
  5. Only a separate selector may promote a passing candidate to a real slot. This repo does not auto-submit.

Why the New File Is Unique

  • Method: BGK-PBE — basement gravity-gradient × radiometric K/eTh enrichment × geodetic dilatation at the piedmont-to-basin transition, NNE-trending normal-fault orientation (Basin-and-Range, extension ψ_ext ≈ 115° WNW–ESE).
  • Not used: no magnetic tilt / Wijns Theta, no dotted-ridge / H19-5 scarp family, no USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue tracing, no thermal-spring / 2 m temperature data.
  • Lineage: byte-distinct from every GEMSDOE24/25/27/28/30/31/32/33/36 submission; no shared dots; no H19-5 or dotted-ridge ancestry.
  • Target: blind, concealed geothermal conduit faults missed by subaerial USGS mapping (sedimented-over intrabasin horst-bounding and relay faults beneath Lake Lahontan clays and piedmont bajadas).
