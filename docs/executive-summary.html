Why 0.2778 is not an explanation or a reliable estimate target

  • The only established reason the GEMSDOE32 number is highest in the supplied list is that 0.2778 is the largest number typed beside the nine sites. The score does not identify the causal feature, and no organizer receipt linking it to the TIFF hash is in this checkout.
  • GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive-cell sets; GEMSDOE36 contains all GEMSDOE32 dots and adds 1,200, while its owner-reported score is lower. Dot count alone does not explain the ordering.
  • The official task uses expert-labeled faults absent from the public USGS database. The local hide-and-recover diagnostic withholds catalogue segments and is not the contest's off-catalogue evaluation. Its values must not be treated as leaderboard projections.
  • The current official public leaderboard was fetched 2026-10-31 UTC: #1 = 0.4544. The stated 0.3195 is not the page's current highest score.

Four research hypotheses, ranked for later validation

No experiments were run in this review; these are qualitative priorities, not DTI estimates.

  1. 1 m DEM paired-scarp profile asymmetry — 1 m DEM profile curvature/paired breaks; potentially moderate–high benefit, high cost, not viable until the official competition DEM link file and coverage are restored. Mimics: channels, roads, terrace risers, tiling artifacts.
  2. Scale-dependent gravity–magnetic edge phase lag — gravity and RTP/TMI layers; moderate potential, medium/high cost. Mimics: lithologic contacts, intrusions, remanence, survey striping.
  3. Signed geodetic dilation zero-crossing networks — signed dilatation, shear, second invariant; low/moderate potential, low/medium cost. Mimics: interpolation seams and broad regional strain.
  4. Gravity-edge plus basement-depth change point with magnetic-negative control — gravity gradients, depth-to-base, magnetic gradient; low/moderate potential, medium cost and lower novelty. Mimics: basin-fill or lithologic boundaries.

Detailed rationale and sources are in the research slate. The protocol's single assigned method paragraph is missing, data/cache and dependencies are absent, and no candidate can be honestly validated yet.
