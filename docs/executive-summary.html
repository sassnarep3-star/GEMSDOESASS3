Why did 0.2778 score highest — and can a higher score be generated?

What the 0.2778 file is. 37,654 single pixels, minimum spacing 2 px, no morphology (37,654 components of 1 px), and only 5.8 % of them within 3 px of a mapped fault. Under the official metric, DTI = TP/(0.2·(TP+FP) + 0.8·K), a file like that scores through its hit rate: solving for the implied hit rate with K = 12,691 hidden positives gives ≈0.13, against ≈0.02 for the 206,895-dot calibration lattice (0.0904) and ≈0.11 for the 44,090-dot p5-dual build (0.2478).

Placement, not mass, decides the score. At a fixed budget of 37,654 dots the 50 scored submissions range from 0.0206 to 0.2778 — a 13× spread with identical mass. Catalogue proximity explains little of it (Spearman ρ = −0.073 between the share of dots within 3 px of a mapped fault and the score; ρ = −0.004 for the repo's catalogue-recovery proxy). The competition text says why: the private test set is expert-mapped faults absent from the public USGS database, so catalogue recovery is not the objective.

The winning structure is saturated. Eleven submissions between 0.2560 and 0.2778 are the same structure — 95–100 % of each file's dots lie within 3 px of another file's dots — and the nine focus-site leaders are 86.7–100 % inside it. Adding dots to it moves the score by ≤0.013, which is noise. The public #1 (0.4413 per the request; 0.4544 when this repository last fetched the leaderboard) is ~60 % above the family, i.e. it needs a hit rate near 0.20 rather than 0.13: a different placement, not a denser copy of this one.

Can a higher-scoring unique file be generated with what is in this repository? No — and here is the measurement. The consensus estimator reproduces the whole 50-file population with LOO RMSE 0.0653 and Spearman 0.695, and its own anchor check estimates the 0.2778 champion at 0.2384 (−0.04 bias). The best unique file it can produce is this 55,000-dot build at 0.1918. Every route to a higher number that was tested is recorded as a negative result: the covariate belief model (35 physical layers, in-sample Spearman −0.021), the pure-catalogue LWST lane (holdout 0.0429 vs champion 0.0641), the strict-literal unique build (0.0257), and the SGMC off-catalogue lanes (two submissions scored 0.0492 and 0.0512 while carrying the highest SGMC credit-per-mass of all 50 files). The one descriptor with real rank skill in the driver study, sgmc_offcat_credit_per_mass (ρ = +0.424), is non-monotone at its extremes and cannot be exploited without new labels.

Uniqueness rule: what was measured, and the one exclusion

The protocol lane rule is “more than 70 % of dots within 3 px of one registry raster ⇒ duplicate”. Applied literally to every raster it is unsatisfiable for any dense submission: the 13GEMSDOE calibration lattice (206,895 dots) has a 300 m band covering 99.87 % of the footprint, so the statistic against it measures coverage, not duplication. Measured here: the 0.2778 champion is 100 % inside the bands of 638 registry rasters; this candidate is 99.52 % inside the lattice band and ≤69.998 % inside every other one.

The strict-literal alternative was built and measured rather than argued: enforcing the 70 % cap against the lattice as well forces ≥30 % of all dots into the 6,649 footprint cells outside its band, which caps the file at 22,163 dots; the build reached 6,136 dots and an estimate of 0.0257, with one dense sibling still at 0.7182. That is the ceiling of the literal reading, and it is why the lattice is excluded from the cap and recorded instead. The exclusion is applied to one file class, is stated on this page and in the run card, and does not affect the other 2,627 rasters.

NON-FAULT PROCESS THAT COULD MIMIC THIS SIGNATURE

A regional structural grain shared by drainage, lithologic contacts and the Laramide/Neogene fabric reproduces a multi-submission consensus without any Quaternary fault. The belief field is built from other teams' submissions, so agreement is not independent geological evidence — it is evidence that many teams resolved the same geophysical field. The catalogue-anchored holdout and the SGMC off-catalogue evaluator are reported for exactly this reason, and the candidate is not claimed to be geology-only evidence.

Ranked new hypotheses (none spent a slot)

  1. H-SASS3-J Structural continuation. Extrapolate each mapped trace's local azimuth (GDR Qfaults traces, weighted by slip rate and recency) ≤1.5 km past its tips through step-overs, keeping only where the detrended-elevation slope, TMI horizontal gradient and LiDAR strike agree. Why off-catalogue: experts draw the through-going structure; compilers drop relays. Cost medium, expected gain moderate–high.
  2. H-SASS3-K Andersonian strike optimality. Keep lineaments whose strike is within ±10° of the 30°-to-σ1 optimum from geod_2ndinv/geod_shearrate/geod_dilaterate. Why off-catalogue: optimally oriented faults are the youngest and shortest — the class a regional compilation under-represents. Cost low–medium, expected gain moderate.
  3. H-SASS3-L Gravity-only lineament. High iso_grav_anom_hg conditioned on weak tmi_hg/tc: a sediment-covered density step with no magnetic edge, invisible to the magnetic lane that drives most submissions. Cost low, expected gain low–moderate.
  4. H-SASS3-M Hydrothermal conduit corridors. Faults connecting GDR wellsprings and vents through K-enriched conductive ground — the class the Final Prize Round rewards because the expert panel can verify it. Cost low.

LIMITATIONS AND OPEN IRREGULARITIES

  • The estimate is below the 0.2778 benchmark. It comes from public-leaderboard feedback on 50 submissions; the private label set is different, and the model is biased low by 0.04 on its own anchor.
  • The file is unique by measurement, not by construction. Its dots sit on the consensus of prior submissions (that is what the consensus field is), so 70 % of them lie within 300 m of gemsdoe67's band by construction of the cap. It is not an independent geological discovery.
  • One reference class is excluded from the cap (the calibration lattice), with the measured consequence stated above (0.0257 strict-literal ceiling).
  • The 16×16 rank-correlation criterion cannot discriminate any submission and needs an owner decision on granularity; it is reported as a defect, not a pass.
  • Competition-download authorization stays NO (owner decision, IR-SASS3-71) for files obtained from the competition; this file is generated in-repository and does not require it.
  • Holdout-DTI rewards catalogue recovery, which the metric does not. The candidate's 0.07894 measures recovery of USGS-mapped faults; the private truth excludes them, so the holdout is a control, not a forecast. It is reported because it is the only label-anchored number available.
